What an Evidence Card for GTA contains

Evidence Cards cover anomaly findings on the EVM networks; anomaly detection is not yet available on this network, so the catalog reports coverage, verified swaps and pools only. For reference, an Evidence Card answers five questions:

  1. What was observed? The metric and its unit, the observed value against the expected baseline, and the window in UTC.
  2. Where is the evidence? Network, chain id and the token's contract; the block range; each measured pool with its protocol and decoder family; the swap count and the five largest transactions; the RPC providers and ingestion runs covering the window.
  3. How was it computed? The method and its version, the baseline window and value (frozen when the anomaly starts), the parameters and the detector's facts.
  4. How much can we trust it? Hours expected against hours fully covered, every gap, the checks that passed, and the caveats and alternative explanations.
  5. How can it be checked independently? The detection SQL with its SHA-256, and a recipe for any public JSON-RPC node that needs no access to our database.
