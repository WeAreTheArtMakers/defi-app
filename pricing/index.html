What you get, and what we don't promise

Every package includes

  • Verified ingestion: a window is published only if its coverage, decoding and uniqueness checks pass
  • Gaps listed, never hidden or zero-filled
  • Anomaly findings with their method, baseline, alternative explanations and the SQL to reproduce them
  • An analyst that only phrases verified facts, using local AI models by default, or your own AI provider key after an explicit data-egress consent
  • Your organisation's data access kept separate from other organisations'

We don't promise

  • Continuous monitoring: tracked tokens are collected and analysed on a 12-hour schedule, so an alert can arrive hours after unusual activity begins
  • Coverage of every venue where a token trades: only the verified, ingested pools are measured
  • Identification of people or entities: addresses are addresses
  • Investment advice or forecasts
  • SLAs, 24/7 support or certifications we don't hold

Questions

How do I pay?

Online (Evidence Snapshot, Research, Team):

  1. Choose a plan at app.wearetheartmakers.com/checkout.
  2. Pay the exact amount shown — in USDC or USDT on Ethereum, Arbitrum One or OP Mainnet, or USDC on Base — or by bank transfer with the reference shown.
  3. Stablecoin payments are activated automatically after network confirmation, and the on-chain transaction is your payment record. Bank transfers are confirmed by us, usually within one business day.
  4. We email you a one-time link to your access key. See the terms of sale.

By invoice (pilots, self-hosted licences, or on request):

  1. You receive an invoice — in the application, or by email — with a unique invoice reference.
  2. You pay by bank transfer and put the invoice reference in the transfer description. The bank details are on the invoice.
  3. You email the transfer receipt to admin@wearetheartmakers.com.
  4. We confirm the payment, usually within one business day, and your organisation's access is activated.

Bank charges are paid by the sender.

Which currency, and is VAT/KDV included?

Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT/KDV. Customers in Türkiye pay in Turkish lira at the CBRT exchange rate on the invoice date; VAT/KDV is added where it applies.

What happens to the $900 Evidence Snapshot fee?

It is credited in full to a pilot signed within 60 days of the Snapshot's delivery. The Snapshot is paid upfront. Some tokens have no pools we can verify, so if you want, email us before you pay and we check your token for free. Online purchases are not refunded if the token turns out to have no verifiable pools.

What exactly do the numbers cover?

Only the networks, protocol families and pools that are verified and ingested for your organisation. Other venues and undiscovered pools are not counted, so no figure covers every venue where a token trades. Pool-event volume counts a routed trade in every ingested pool it passes through. Findings are statistical observations; they do not establish intent or wrongdoing.

Where is my data hosted?

WATAM-hosted pilots and plans run on WATAM's own server, accessed over HTTPS through Cloudflare, where each organisation sees only its own data. There is no SLA or 24/7 support, and the hosted service is not an independently audited multi-tenant SaaS. With a self-hosted pilot or licence, everything runs on your own infrastructure and your backups are encrypted with a key you hold. We never ask for confidential datasets, private keys or passwords.

When is my Evidence Snapshot delivered?

Within 2 business days of a confirmed payment, for a token that is available in our catalog. A token we don't cover yet first needs about 7 days of collection; on BNB Smart Chain collection runs forward only, so only days collected after the token is added are available. Access runs 7 days from payment confirmation, not from delivery, so if your token is not Available yet, wait until it is before you pay. Ask for a free token check first.

How do I get support?

Email admin@wearetheartmakers.com, Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 Türkiye time (UTC+3), in English or Turkish. We reply within one business day. There is no SLA or 24/7 support.

Can I start without a Snapshot?

Yes. After the free demo you can go straight to a pilot. The Snapshot exists for teams that want to see results on their own token before a larger commitment.
