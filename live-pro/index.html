Six checks, every 5 minutes, on the last 15 minutes

Each rule is compared with your token's own history, not a fixed market-wide number. The baseline is the median of the verified, covered hours of the last 7 days, scaled to 15 minutes.

Large trade

One swap worth at least your threshold. The minimum is $1,000.We suggest one per token: the top 0.5% of its single trades over the last 7 days (usually $1,000–5,000). High severity at 4× your threshold.

What it is for
Seeing a big buy or sell as it happens, with the trader's address and the router it went through.
Who cares

  • Funds

Volume burst

Valued volume in 15 minutes is at least max(5× baseline, $5,000), or the swap count is at least max(5× baseline, 30).

Trading went quiet

Swaps fall to 10% of the baseline or less.Only for tokens whose baseline is at least 20 swaps per 15 minutes.

One-sided flow

At least 95% of the token volume is on one side (buys or sells), over at least 10 swaps, with valued volume of at least max(baseline, $2,000).Only if the token's usual hourly one-sidedness is below 80%.

One address dominates

One receiving address takes at least 80% of the token volume, over at least 5 swaps, with valued volume of at least max(baseline, $2,000).Only if the token's usual top-recipient share is below 50%. High severity at 95%.

What it is for

Pool liquidity drop

Both token balances of a pool are at least 50% below their median of the previous 6 hours (at least 3 readings).Both sides must drop: a swap moves the balances in opposite directions, a withdrawal lowers both. Uniswap v4 and PancakeSwap Infinity pools are not checked.

What it is for
Knowing when liquidity leaves one of your pools, which makes every later trade move the price more.

What this alert does not mean

  • It does not say anyone did something wrong. It is a statistical observation, not a claim of intent or manipulation.
  • It does not identify a person. The receiving address is often a router, an aggregator or a market maker; the behaviour label is a rule over counts, not an identification.
  • It covers only the watched pools, not LINK's whole market.
  • It is not a signal to buy or sell.

Questions

What counts as confirmed?

A pre-alert is confirmed only when the verified swaps it rests on are the same as the live ones: the same transactions, the same log positions and the same raw amounts. Any difference makes it not confirmed, the difference is recorded, and you get a correction. A liquidity drop is confirmed when a second RPC endpoint returns the same two balances at the same block. If no verified data covers the window within 7 days, it is marked not verifiable.

How fast is a pre-alert, and how fast is the verdict?

A pre-alert usually arrives a few minutes after the trade: about 1–9 minutes depending on the network (see the table above). In our first measured tests, the trade-to-email time was 5.4 minutes on Base and 8.6 minutes on BNB Smart Chain. The verdict comes when our verified run covers the window, usually 12–36 hours later.

Which networks and tokens?

EVM tokens in our catalog, on networks such as Ethereum, Arbitrum One, OP Mainnet, Base and BNB Smart Chain. Live Pro watches the token's verified pools; you can see the list of pools for each watched token in the app. Solana and TON tokens get verified reports but no pre-alerts.

What about false positives and automated traders?

Every pre-alert is checked against verified data, and we track the share that is not confirmed. Thresholds are relative to your token's own baseline, and checks like "one address dominates" stay silent for tokens where that pattern is normal. Many alerts involve routers, aggregators or market-making bots; the address card says so, with the facts behind the label, and shows whether the activity is unusual for that address. A busy bot doing what it always does is still worth knowing about, but it is not news.

Do I need an AI subscription to ask questions?

No. The four preset questions, in English and Turkish, are answered directly from the alert's facts with no AI model. For your own questions, your organization can connect its own provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a self-hosted model). Answers must match the alert's facts, and no answer gives advice.

What happens to our data?

Live Pro uses public on-chain data only. Your alerts are visible only to your organization. Pre-alerts are kept; the raw live swaps behind them are kept for 35 days. Every CSV export is written to an audit log. If you connect your own AI provider, an organization administrator must first accept that the alert's facts are sent to that provider under your own account.
